# Glossary

## Branch
An operational business branch of Baithul Madeena. Most ERP data is scoped to a branch.

## Super Admin
A privileged user who can access all branches and switch branch context.

## Customer
A person or organization represented in the ERP. A customer may act as an owner, tenant, or both.

## Owner
A customer who owns property managed or leased by Baithul Madeena.

## Tenant
A customer who leases property or a rentable component from Baithul Madeena.

## Property
A real-estate asset such as an apartment building, villa, shop, office, space, labor camp, warehouse, or land.

## Rentable Component
A separately rentable part of a property, such as a unit, room, area, or bed.

## Owner Agreement
Agreement through which Baithul Madeena takes a property or asset scope from an owner.

## Tenant Agreement
Agreement through which Baithul Madeena leases a property or rentable component to a tenant.

## Installment
A scheduled payment amount under an agreement.

## Cash Receipt
Receipt generated for inward revenue/payment received.

## Purchase Receipt
Receipt generated for outward revenue/payment made.

## Work Order
Maintenance job raised for a property or rentable component.

## Service Charge
Service/labor charge associated with maintenance work.

## Inventory Item
Stocked item used in maintenance or operations.

## Stock Movement
Auditable inventory quantity change.

## Purchase Order
Order placed with a vendor to procure inventory or other goods.

## Scrap
Inventory written off because it is unusable, obsolete, damaged, or intentionally disposed of.

## Invoice
Currently independent billing document with header and line items, not required to be linked to other ERP domains.
